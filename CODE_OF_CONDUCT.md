# Code of Conduct

> **Project:** Scrobblix  
> **Owner:** Rahul Muraleedharan (`frpboy`)  
> **Status:** Documentation baseline  
> **Last updated:** 2026-08-01


Scrobblix uses the Contributor Covenant principles: be respectful, assume good intent while requiring technical rigor, reject harassment and discrimination, keep criticism focused on work, and protect private information shared during debugging.

## Expected behavior

- Give reproducible technical feedback.
- Distinguish facts, measurements, assumptions, and preferences.
- Respect maintainers' decisions after documented review.
- Never publish another person's listening history, credentials, logs, or diagnostic bundles without permission.
- Avoid gatekeeping based on experience, language, geography, identity, or tool preference.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, discriminatory language, credential disclosure, malware submissions, and misuse of project infrastructure are prohibited.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the security/contact method published in the repository. The maintainer may edit or remove content, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation depending on severity.
