# v1 Release Plan

> **Project:** Scrobblix

> **Last updated:** 2026-08-01

## Scope discipline

v1 proves trustworthy Windows/browser Last.fm tracking and diagnostics. It does not require every proposed analytics chart, player plugin, site, or cross-platform feature.

## Suggested cadence

- Internal prototype builds continuously.
- Alpha releases after M1/M2 to technical testers.
- Beta after Native Host/browser packaging and privacy review.
- Release candidate after feature freeze, full migration/compatibility/security/accessibility pass.

## Exit conditions

Use `01_Product/RELEASE_CRITERIA.md` and `12_Testing/RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`. Timeline dates should be set only after M0 estimates and maintainer availability; do not invent commitments before evidence.
