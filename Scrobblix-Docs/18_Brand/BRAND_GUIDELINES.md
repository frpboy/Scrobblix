# Brand Guidelines

## Brand idea

Scrobblix represents reliable, transparent listening history. The brand should feel precise, calm, modern, and technically trustworthy rather than nostalgic, noisy, or “hacker themed.”

## Name usage

- Product name: **Scrobblix**.
- Repository slug: `scrobblix`.
- Publisher/maintainer attribution: **Rahul Muraleedharan (`frpboy`)**.
- Do not label the project as a Zabnix product or use Zabnix branding.
- Do not imply sponsorship or endorsement by Last.fm, Microsoft, browser vendors, or streaming services.

## Voice

Use direct, diagnostic language. Prefer “Waiting for 47 seconds of confirmed playback” over “Not ready.” Prefer “Last.fm rejected the session key; reconnect your account” over “Something went wrong.”

Avoid blame, jokes in error states, exaggerated privacy claims, and status labels without evidence.

## Brand attributes

- Reliable.
- Explainable.
- Private by default.
- Lightweight.
- Native to Windows.
- Music-focused without imitating a streaming service.

## Trademark and third-party names

Third-party names and logos are used only to identify compatibility or integration. Follow their current brand and attribution rules. Product launch is blocked until name and mark clearance is documented in [`NAME_IP_REVIEW.md`](NAME_IP_REVIEW.md).
